# Kairos Materials

Mathacker-06Lx is an equivariant message-passing interatomic potential by Kairos Materials
(62.3M parameters, 10 layers, lmax 4, dual cutoffs 3/7 A) pretrained on MPtrj, OMat24,
sAlex and ELEMENTA, and fine-tuned for lattice thermal conductivity on Gaussian-displacement
fc2/fc3 frames with full-set replay.

Code: [kairosmaterial/prophet](https://github.com/kairosmaterial/prophet)
